# Phosphor 0.4.0

Selected aircraft now show photographs from Planespotters.net when online enrichment is enabled.
Photos appear in Phosphor green; hover over a photo or focus it with the keyboard to reveal its original colour.
Turn on Show aircraft photos in full colour in Settings to keep them in colour.

The PHOTO section expands when a photograph is available and remembers your choice to collapse it while that aircraft remains selected.
Photographer credit appears below the image, and clicking the photo opens its original page on Planespotters.net.
Photos are fetched directly and are not stored on disk.

Install this update through Check for Updates in the Phosphor menu, or download the app archive.
Existing Phosphor settings and cached aircraft details are preserved.
